what data is collected
some data is collected now. we collect basic info associated with your account to ensure the website, games and tools are functional. this may include: email addresses, hashed passwords, user login activity.
applications installed on your device may require profiling of the devices hardware and software components (e.g, cpu, gpu, ram, operating system, graphics drivers) to function. if you're not ok with that, throw any devices you may happen to own in any body of water of your choosing
why is it collected
functionality.
my mission is not mass surveilance, its to make things that work. the data collected is generally not collected for any purpose that does not involve making the stuff actually work.
reasonable measures are also in place to prevent the very limited sensitive data we have from leaked (e.g, password hashing and salting), but do note that no system is perfect. avoid reusing passwords as much as possible, don't trust me to have flawless security.
